Project Nestlings

Making green accessible to all children.

Accessible Holistic Green Education for Children

Why?

Children are our next generation, and we believe that caring for the planet starts with early awareness. Yet, not every child has equal access to green education. Many schools focus on grades, not green habits, and sustainability often stays out of reach for those without the resources or exposure. Our goal is to make it simple, hands-on, and part of everyday learning-so every child can grow up caring for the world they live in.

What we do?

  • Education, Equip, Empower
  • Only through learning can children understand their impact, gain the skills to care for the planet, and build the confidence to lead small & big changes at home and in school.
